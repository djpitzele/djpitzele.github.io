Co-led a project that aims to investigate the capabilities of various methods of trajectory conditioning for robot policies. 3D trajectories are a strong intermediate modality for robot manipulation, and can often help models generalize into out-of-distribution scenes or tasks. We compared various methods of trajectory conditioning on both diffusion policy and VLA-based architectures.
